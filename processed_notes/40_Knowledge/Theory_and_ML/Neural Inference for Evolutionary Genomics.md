# Neural Inference for Evolutionary Genomics

## Conceptual Framework
- Replaces computationally intractable likelihood functions with amortized neural inference (e.g. Graph Neural Networks or Diffusion Models trained on evolutionary simulations).
- Maps sequence distance matrices and topological embeddings directly to evolutionary parameters (mutation rates, selection coefficients, branch lengths).
