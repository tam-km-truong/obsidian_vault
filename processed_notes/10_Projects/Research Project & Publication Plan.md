## 3. Scientific Writing Workflow & Principles
1. **Hierarchy of Technical Details:**
   - Abstract & Intro: High-level motivation (storage sustainability, billion-dollar archival problem).
   - Core Methods: Rigorous formalization of order-preserving bin packing.
   - Experiments: Clear empirical comparison against gzip, AGC, and MBGC.
2. **Semi-Complete Figures First:**
   - Draft pipeline schematics and benchmark Pareto curves early to align co-authors before drafting prose.
3. **Dimensional Linearization:**
   - Deconstruct complex multidimensional pipelines into clear, linear narrative arcs.
