# Bioinformatics Databases & Resources Overview

## The NAR Database Issue
Every January, *Nucleic Acids Research* (NAR) publishes a dedicated database issue summarizing active, curated molecular biology databases worldwide.

## Core Databases in Vault Workflows
- **NCBI / GenBank / RefSeq:** Primary nucleotide and assembly archives.
- **GTDB (Genome Taxonomy Database):** Standardized, phylogenetically consistent bacterial and archaeal taxonomy.
- **AllTheBacteria:** Uniformly assembled isolate collection (1.9M assemblies).
- **ChEMBL / PubChem:** Bioactive molecules and targets.

## Guiding Philosophy (from Biostar Handbook)
> *"Make your data small, analyze it quickly, and ask quick and easy questions—but lots of them. You will make discoveries faster by testing many focused hypotheses."*
