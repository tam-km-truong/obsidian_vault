# Vertical and Horizontal Gene Transfer in Bacterial Genomics

## 1. Mechanisms of Inheritance
- **Vertical Inheritance:** Transmission of genetic material from parent to offspring during binary fission. Generates clonal phylogenetic tree structures.
- **Horizontal Gene Transfer (HGT):** Lateral transfer of genetic material between distinct lineages via:
  1. *Conjugation:* Direct plasmid transfer via pili.
  2. *Transformation:* Uptake of free naked DNA from the environment.
  3. *Transduction:* Bacteriophage-mediated DNA transfer.

---

## 2. Implications for Pangenome Compression
- Vertical inheritance creates deep syntenic conservation, enabling high compression ratios when traversing branches.
- HGT introduces accessory mobile elements (plasmids, genomic islands) that violate strict tree hierarchies, creating accessory pangenome variation that must be handled during batch bin packing.
